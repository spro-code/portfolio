Surface container for grouping content. The default raised card; `flat` removes shadow, `sunk` recesses, `interactive` adds a hover lift.

```jsx
<Card interactive>
  <h3 className="as-title-3">Reading fluency</h3>
  <p className="as-body">87% of trials met this week.</p>
</Card>
```

`pad`: `sm | md | lg`.
